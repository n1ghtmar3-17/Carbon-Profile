Carbon Profile

Since the industrial revolution in the 19th century, the earth has experienced rapid increase in temperature due to the emissions of greenhouse gases. Some causes could include the burning of fossil fuels and deforestation, which emits carbon dioxide, or the raising of livestock, releasing methane. The warming earth leads to climate change, including more extreme weather, droughts, and rising sea levels.

Did You Know?

  1. 2024 has been the hottest year recorded, with temperatures 2.30 degrees Fahrenheit above the 20th century average, beating the previous record in 2023.
  2. 3.6 billion people live in areas with high risk of effects from climate change.
  3. Globally, forests absorb nearly 16 billion metric tonnes of carbon dioxide per year. In the U.S. alone, forests offset about 16% of the country's greenhouse gas emissions.
